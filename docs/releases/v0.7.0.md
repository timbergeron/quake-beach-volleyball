# v0.7.0 — Dense anatomy and first-person hands

This release packages all five dense MD3s, the continuous anatomical athletes,
first-person hands, current textures, compiled QuakeC/HUD, court and sounds.
The two animation reviewers and a ready-to-edit texture painting kit are
separate downloads. It includes the anatomical-hand and directional-cut work
that followed v0.6.0, plus the QSS-M density upgrade.

## Models and animation

| Model | Triangles | Poses |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Home athlete | 35,422 | 389 |
| Away athlete | 35,422 | 389 |
| First-person hands | 21,814 | 402 |
| Volleyball | 20,544 | 1 |
| Net and equipment | 88,736 | 37 |

The Norway-inspired male athletes use continuous CC0 anatomical geometry,
dual quaternion skinning, navy/red clothing artwork, bare feet, sun caps and
full mirrored shield sunglasses. The first-person rig has individual finger
joints, opposed thumbs and an anatomical skin atlas. Refinement and crease
plans preserve every exported pose on MD3's fixed position grid.

The body library retains 31 clips and 389 poses at 24 Hz. Hands retain those
core ranges and add 13 wrist-away poses, for 32 clips and 402 poses. Deep-dish
setting yields through fingers and wrists; float serving uses a firm palm;
inward and wrist-away cuts have distinct follow-throughs. Approach backswing,
bow-and-arrow loading, attacks, locomotion, digs and recovery remain connected
to the existing game states. Back/jump sets, pike blocks, lateral presses,
peeling and pancakes are available library variants; separate block rules and
new gameplay inputs remain future work.

The ball has a refined icosphere silhouette. Net cords, posts and fittings have
denser rounded geometry while retaining all 37 recoil poses. Contact physics,
equipment collision and existing animation timing are unchanged by density.

## Downloads

- [beachvolley.zip](https://github.com/timbergeron/quake-beach-volleyball/releases/download/v0.7.0/beachvolley.zip): playable mod, current skins, both manifests, documentation and native render evidence.
- [player-animation-review.html](https://github.com/timbergeron/quake-beach-volleyball/releases/download/v0.7.0/player-animation-review.html): offline body review with 31 clips, kit switching, orbiting, scrubbing and stroke playback.
- [hand-animation-review.html](https://github.com/timbergeron/quake-beach-volleyball/releases/download/v0.7.0/hand-animation-review.html): offline first-person/anatomy review with all 32 clips, including both cut directions.
- [texture-paint-kit.zip](https://github.com/timbergeron/quake-beach-volleyball/releases/download/v0.7.0/texture-paint-kit.zip): six lossless PNG atlases, UV guides, material-region overlays, editing prompts and licenses.
- [SHA256SUMS](https://github.com/timbergeron/quake-beach-volleyball/releases/download/v0.7.0/SHA256SUMS): checksums for all four downloads.

The reviewers embed compressed copies of the exact MD3 bytes and need a browser
with WebGL and `DecompressionStream`. Unzip the texture kit and open its
`index.html`. The kit contains the existing validated textures; its proposed
new texture artwork has not been generated yet.

Unpack the playable ZIP into your Quake directory and launch QSS-M:

```sh
quakespasm -game beachvolley -fsaa 4 +exec beach.cfg +map beach
```

An installed copy of Quake including `id1/pak0.pak` is required. The release
contains no base Quake data or engine/tool executables.

## Validation and practical budget

The unchanged delivered model/game bytes have strict checks for all five MD3s,
including every 389-pose body, 402-pose hand and 37-pose net sample. Their build
passed 22 Python asset/launcher tests and 257 gameplay assertions in the actual
QC VM. Live float serving, both receive/set/attack doubles fixtures, net recoil
and equipment views passed in QSS-M. Nine native body captures, seven
first-person captures and four ball views identify the model/skin/engine hashes.
Both browser reviewers exercised every clip without errors.

All five models loaded together with the default 384 MiB heap. The separate
model cache grew to a 409 MiB budget and reported 288.4 MiB used/peak with zero
evictions. Three visible athletes, hands, ball and net total 237,360 triangles
before culling. Pose VBOs require about 268.2 MiB across the distinct loaded
models, before textures and other GPU allocations. Target GPU frame rates have
not been benchmarked. See [density notes](../model-detail.md),
[inventory](../model-quality/inventory.json) and
[engine evidence](../model-quality/game-summary.json).

The texture exporter adds four focused regressions; the harness suite now has
40 passing tests. Its six generated PNGs match the source TGA pixels exactly.
The release archives are checked for CRCs, delivered model/skin bytes,
manifests, compiled QC, licenses and required asset paths. Checksums cover the
playable archive, both reviewers and texture kit.

Reusable UV-kit export and [texture-authoring lessons](https://github.com/timbergeron/md3harness/blob/5b2cf175a45bb1b773230b7cf4660272672cf0b2/docs/texture-authoring.md)
are committed to MD3 harness, alongside its
[QSS-M density profile](https://github.com/timbergeron/md3harness/blob/5b2cf175a45bb1b773230b7cf4660272672cf0b2/docs/qssm-limits.md).
