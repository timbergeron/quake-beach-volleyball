# v0.6.0 — Beach equipment and animated athletes

The playable ZIP includes the new ball, net, court textures, male players and
first-person arms alongside the compiled game, HUD, court map, sounds and
documentation. Existing float/jump serves, skilled receiving and doubles rallies
remain available.

## New assets and behavior

- Original MD3 volleyball with curved panels, seams, valve and a 512×512 atlas.
  The game renders rotation around the physical spin axis.
- Animated MD3 beach net with 37 poses, open cord geometry, cloth bands, padded
  posts, antennas, tension fittings and a 1024×1024 atlas. Mesh/band impacts
  animate a local recoil; posts and antennas participate in collision, and
  antenna faults reach the HUD and scoring.
- Prepared sand and ocean textures, court markings and external texture
  replacements used by the compiled beach map.
- Original Mol/Sørum-inspired male athletes in navy and red Norway kits, with
  full mirrored shield sunglasses, sun caps and bare feet. Both body variants
  and the separate first-person arms ship with their textures and clip manifest.
- 31 clips / 389 poses sampled at 24 Hz: deep hand setting, two-arm approach
  backswing, takeoff, bow-and-arrow loading, spike/roll follow-through, float and
  jump serving, directional footwork, dives, getting up and landing. Core clips
  are connected to existing human/bot states, with Quake interpolation and
  camera framing for first-person arms. The HUD identifies the red opponents.

The library also contains back/jump sets, cuts, pokeys, split steps, pike blocks
with lateral presses, peeling and pancakes. These are authored animation clips;
their separate gameplay inputs and block-contact rules remain future work.

The player models each have 5,004 triangles. The first-person arms have 1,496.
The playable archive contains both kit atlases and
`progs/bv_athlete.animations.json`, preserving the shader paths and frame ranges.
See [the player notes](../player.md) for authoring and review commands.

## Downloads and installation

Download [beachvolley.zip](https://github.com/timbergeron/quake-beach-volleyball/releases/download/v0.6.0/beachvolley.zip)
and unpack it into your Quake directory. Launch QSS-M:

```sh
quakespasm -game beachvolley -fsaa 4 +exec beach.cfg +map beach
```

An installed copy of Quake with `id1/pak0.pak` is required. No Quake PAKs or
engine/tool executables are bundled.

The optional [player-animation-review.html](https://github.com/timbergeron/quake-beach-volleyball/releases/download/v0.6.0/player-animation-review.html)
is an offline WebGL review of the exact exported player bytes. Open it in a
browser to choose clips or full strokes, slow playback, scrub, orbit and switch
kits. It is distributed separately from the playable ZIP. `SHA256SUMS` covers
both downloads.

## Validation

- 249 gameplay assertions passed inside the real QC VM.
- 14 launcher and ball/net/player asset tests passed.
- Both body kits and the final arms passed strict MD3 checks in all 389 poses;
  the net passed its 37-pose contract.
- 11 actual QSS-M player captures passed with paired empty-studio baselines.
  The offline review exercised all 31 clips and both kit textures without
  browser errors.
- Final-build fixtures passed for gameplay, court/HUD rendering, a live serve,
  the human-set doubles rally and the opponent jump-topspin rally. Earlier
  integration checks also covered receiving and emergency dives at 50/100 Hz.
- The ZIP is checked for CRCs, matching source model/texture bytes, compiled
  QC, configuration and required asset paths. Obsolete ball/athlete/hand MDLs
  and Quake PAKs are excluded.

Reusable character production lessons and regression coverage are committed to
[md3harness](https://github.com/timbergeron/md3harness/blob/74699129b457265606c9803cbd7c12739e759f31/docs/character-animation.md).
